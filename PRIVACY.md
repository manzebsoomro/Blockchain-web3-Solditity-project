# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated**: May 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mint Experiment Site ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our website and services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

**Email Address**
- Collected during account registration for authentication
- Used for email verification via OTP
- Used for transaction confirmations and notifications

**Wallet Address**
- Ethereum wallet address connected to your account
- Used for minting and token transfer operations
- Stored securely in our database

**Transaction Data**
- Mint requests and transaction history
- Amount of tokens minted
- Blockchain transaction details

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data**
- IP address (for rate limiting and security)
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referral source

**Cookies**
- Session cookies for authentication
- No tracking cookies or third-party cookies
- Cookies are httpOnly and Secure

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- **Authentication**: Verify your identity and manage your account
- **Service Delivery**: Process mint requests and transactions
- **Communication**: Send transaction confirmations and important updates
- **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud and abuse
- **Compliance**: Meet legal and regulatory requirements

### 3.2 Legal Basis
- **Consent**: You consent to data collection by using our service
- **Contract**: We need your data to provide the service
- **Legal Obligation**: We may need to disclose data to comply with law
- **Legitimate Interest**: We use data to improve security and service quality

## 4. Data Retention

### 4.1 Retention Periods
- **Email Address**: Retained for account duration + 30 days after deletion
- **Wallet Address**: Retained for account duration + 30 days after deletion
- **Transaction History**: Retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 1 year
- **Session Data**: Deleted after 30 minutes of inactivity

### 4.2 Data Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We will:
1. Delete your email and wallet information within 30 days
2. Anonymize transaction history (retain for regulatory compliance)
3. Delete session and authentication data immediately
4. Retain audit logs as required by law

### 5.1 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- **Access Control**: Strict access controls on all systems
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication support
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and logging
- **Incident Response**: Rapid response to security incidents

### 5.2 Your Responsibilities
- Keep your email and password confidential
- Never share your wallet private keys
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication when available
- Report suspicious activity immediately
